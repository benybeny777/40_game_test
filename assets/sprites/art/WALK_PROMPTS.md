# 歩行専用素材（2026-10-29）

image_genで既存の人物像を参照し、歩行用に別PNGを生成した。指示は6行だったが採用出力は5行であるため、実装は各人物5コマとして扱い、6コマ生成済みとは記載しない。Aは5列×5行、Bは5列×5行、Cは7列×5行。表情・衣装・攻撃素材は既存の正本を維持する。生成後の素材をPython等で描き直していない。

## walk-a

参照と保存先はCREDITS.mdを参照。

Create a PRODUCTION WALK-CYCLE SPRITE ATLAS for this existing pixel-art RPG. These references identify the characters and style; preserve their identity, costumes, palettes and proportions exactly, but DRAW NEW LEG ANIMATION, not repeated idle poses. 5 columns left to right: the navy samurai with red scarf and sword held sheathed/low, silver-haired teal scarf ninja, black-and-pink twin-tail goth girl with wand held close, brown-haired glasses otaku with backpack and glowsticks held close, blonde burgundy jacket gambler holding coin.
STRICT GRID: 5 EQUAL columns, SIX EQUAL rows. One character per column, same character through all six rows. Total 30 isolated sprites. Uniform gutters, fully transparent background, no grid lines, no words. High resolution atlas. Body centered horizontally in every cell, consistent body scale and identical ground baseline across rows; keep every part inside its own cell.
ALL face screen RIGHT in a consistent three-quarter side view. These are traversal walk poses, NOT attacks. Six rows in time order: 1 LEFT FOOT forward heel contact / right foot behind toe pushes off, 2 left knee bends absorbing weight, right leg swinging forward behind, 3 RIGHT knee lifted passing left support leg with feet narrowly crossing, 4 RIGHT FOOT forward heel contact / left foot behind toe pushes off, 5 right knee bends absorbing weight left leg swinging, 6 LEFT knee lifted passing right support leg. Feet and knees MUST visibly change between every row: no two rows identical. Counter-swing arms slightly with carried objects held securely, scarf/hair and clothing trail opposite motion. Body lowers only subtly in rows2/5. Clear crisp single pixel clusters, restrained hand-drawn 16-bit console sprite art. No glowy aura, no cast shadows, no platform, no frame scaling, no blurry AI paint.
Special locomotion: jiangshi keeps both legs together and visibly crouches, pushes off, floats up, hangs, descends, lands through six rows; nun long skirt still shows alternate shoes at hem; cardboard old man's thin feet must walk visibly under rigid square box, slightly stooped; sumo heavy waddling weight shifts with alternate lifted foot; frail peasant timid shuffling with bamboo steady; bookworm distracted uneven steps. Never add armor/shields and do not beautify the old man.
